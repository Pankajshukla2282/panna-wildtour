## 10) Ready-To-Use FAQ (Production)

1. What is the best time to visit Panna Tiger Reserve?
The best season is typically from October to June, with different wildlife activity patterns across months.

2. Can tiger sighting be guaranteed?
No. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, but proper slot and zone planning improves your chances.

3. How many safaris should I book for a good experience?
Most travelers prefer at least 2 safari slots for a better overall experience.

4. Are safari permits included in packages?
Permit handling can be included based on the package and selected options.

5. What details are needed to book safari permits?
Traveler name, age, ID details, and preferred safari date/slot are usually required.

6. Do you offer custom packages?
Yes, packages can be customized based on budget, trip duration, and traveler profile.

7. Is transport available?
Yes, transport can be arranged on request depending on route and vehicle availability.

8. Are family-friendly packages available?
Yes, we provide family-oriented plans with balanced timings and stay options.

9. Do you help with last-minute travel?
Yes, subject to safari slot and stay availability.

10. Which safari slot is better, morning or evening?
Both are good. Choice depends on season, availability, and your travel schedule.

11. Do you provide stay booking support?
Yes, we help with suitable stay options as per your comfort and budget.

12. Are meals included in every package?
Meal inclusion depends on selected package and accommodation policy.

13. Is Panna suitable for first-time wildlife travelers?
Yes, Panna is a good destination for first-time safari experiences.

14. Can we combine Panna with nearby sightseeing?
Yes, we can add nearby attractions into multi-day itineraries.

15. Do you provide itinerary before payment?
Yes, a tentative plan can be shared before final confirmation.

16. What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellation terms depend on permit and stay vendor policies and are shared at booking.

17. Is WhatsApp support available?
Yes, WhatsApp support is available for quick planning and updates.

18. Are child and senior-friendly plans available?
Yes, itineraries can be adjusted for comfort and travel pace.

19. Is photography guidance available?
Yes, we suggest slots and route strategy suitable for wildlife photography.

20. How early should I book?
Early booking is recommended, especially for peak season and weekend dates.
